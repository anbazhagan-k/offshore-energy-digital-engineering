## 1. Role purpose

Turn maintenance, condition-monitoring and control-system data into decisions that improve the safety, reliability and availability of Seadrill's fleet. The engineer analyses machinery condition, diagnoses developing faults, identifies recurring failure patterns and recommends maintenance actions. The role also supports Maximo, asset lifecycle management, reliability analytics and AI-assisted decision tools.

## 2. Responsibilities captured from the vacancy

### Maintenance management (PDF pages 1–2)
- Support consistent use of Maximo across the fleet.
- Provide maintenance, condition-monitoring and EAM technical support to onshore and offshore personnel.
- Implement approved maintenance and reliability improvements through management of change.
- Support the Asset Lifecycle Management (ALCM) programme using equipment condition, failure and performance data.

### Condition monitoring (PDF pages 2–3)
- Review vibration spectra, time waveforms, envelope / demodulation and trends.
- Diagnose machinery faults including unbalance, misalignment, looseness, bearing and gear defects, lubrication issues and resonance.
- Prepare condition-monitoring reports with severity, diagnosis and recommended actions.
- Track recommendations through Maximo work orders and verify effectiveness with follow-up measurements.
- Maintain vibration-monitoring databases, machine hierarchies, measurement points, setups, alarm limits and route schedules.
- Review lubricant analysis results for contamination, wear and degradation.
- Conduct onsite measurements and tests such as phase analysis, impact / bump testing and run-up / coast-down testing.
- Coordinate with service providers and equipment manufacturers.

### Data analytics and decision support (PDF pages 2–3)
- Extract, clean and combine data from Maximo, control systems, historians and condition-monitoring databases.
- Improve failure-reporting data quality, including failure mode, mechanism and cause coding and asset hierarchy consistency.
- Develop reliability KPIs and dashboards, including bad actors, MTBF, repeat failures, condition-based maintenance coverage and recommendation closure rate.
- Apply Weibull analysis, failure trending and P-F interval estimation.
- Correlate vibration evidence with control-system data such as speed, load and temperature.
- Support root-cause analysis and corrective-action verification.
- Help develop AI-supported condition-monitoring decision tools: define use cases, prepare and label historical fault data, validate model outputs and work with technology teams on deployment.

### Training and general (PDF page 3)
- Train onshore and offshore personnel in maintenance processes, Maximo use, vibration data collection, sensor mounting and route execution.
- Maintain required documentation, certifications, medicals and offshore safety training.

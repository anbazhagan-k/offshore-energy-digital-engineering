# Dream Jobs

A structured reference library built from two target vacancies to identify the capabilities, tools, standards, qualifications and certifications associated with senior digital engineering and offshore reliability roles.

## Vacancies captured

1. [Senior Digital Project Engineer — Orion Group](01-orion-senior-digital-project-engineer.md)
2. [Condition Monitoring & Reliability Analytics Engineer — Seadrill](02-seadrill-condition-monitoring-reliability-analytics-engineer.md)
3. [Combined skills and certification roadmap](03-combined-skills-and-certification-matrix.md)

## How to use this folder

- Treat each vacancy's mandatory requirements separately from its preferred or desirable criteria.
- Distinguish certifications explicitly mentioned by the employer from training topics or tools. A software product is not a certification.
- Use the combined matrix to plan learning, portfolio evidence and future applications.
- Recheck the live vacancy before applying; job descriptions, availability, eligibility and certification rules can change.

## Important eligibility note

The Seadrill vacancy is based in Houston and states that applicants must be US citizens or already authorised to work in the United States without Seadrill sponsorship. This is a stated eligibility condition, not a skill gap.

# Senior Digital Project Engineer — Orion Group

**Vacancy reference:** 4640  
**Role family:** Digital engineering / digital transformation / project development  
**Source:** Orion Group job listing reviewed in this conversation. Verify the live listing for current wording and status.

## 1. Role purpose

The role is centred on identifying, shaping and preparing digital initiatives in an engineering / energy environment. It combines business engagement, requirements definition, project readiness, commercial assessment, vendor coordination and governance. It is not a conventional discipline-design engineering role.

## 2. Responsibilities and capability areas

### Digital opportunity identification
- Identify business and operational problems that may be addressed through digital solutions.
- Assess potential digital initiatives and articulate the value of proposed solutions.
- Understand digital technology options and their relevance to energy / engineering operations.

### Business analysis and requirements
- Engage stakeholders and conduct workshops.
- Elicit, analyse, document and validate business requirements.
- Translate business needs into requirements, scopes, deliverables and solution proposals.
- Communicate across business, engineering, operations and technology teams.

### Project definition and readiness
- Develop project scope, objectives, deliverables, milestones and roadmaps.
- Identify dependencies, risks, assumptions and constraints.
- Prepare project documentation and readiness material before delivery.
- Understand Agile, software development lifecycle (SDLC) and product / project delivery practices.

### Commercial and governance
- Support cost estimates, financial forecasts and business cases.
- Consider benefits, value, investment requirements and delivery options.
- Prepare statements of work (SOWs) and project proposals.
- Support budget, schedule, risk, cybersecurity and governance reviews.

### Vendor and delivery coordination
- Evaluate vendor proposals and technical / commercial submissions.
- Coordinate third-party providers and monitor deliverables, quality and progress.
- Work with delivery teams to support controlled execution and handover.

## 3. Skills to capture

### Business analysis

- Risk, issue and change management.
- Agile / Scrum concepts and SDLC.
- Project governance and delivery assurance.

### Commercial and communication
- Business case development and benefits realisation.
- Cost estimation and financial forecasting.
- CAPEX / OPEX concepts and ROI.
- SOW preparation and vendor evaluation.
- Executive presentations and decision papers.
- Cross-functional communication and negotiation.

## 5. Certifications and formal qualifications

The vacancy material reviewed did not establish a single mandatory named professional certification. The following are **relevant development options**, not confirmed employer-mandated credentials:

- Project Management Professional (PMP) — project leadership, subject to eligibility.
- PRINCE2 — project management method.
- Professional Scrum Master (PSM I) or Certified ScrumMaster (CSM) — Agile / Scrum fundamentals.
- IIBA ECBA / CCBA / CBAP — business analysis, selected according to experience and eligibility.
- Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) — cloud foundation.
- Microsoft Azure Administrator / Architect learning paths — only if the target role requires deeper Azure capability.
- Prosci Change Management certification — change adoption and organisational change, if relevant.

Do not present these as requirements of the Orion vacancy unless the employer confirms them.

## 8. Candidate fit considerations

Transferable strengths may include engineering education, ERP product documentation, technical communication, stakeholder coordination and experience explaining complex systems.

Potential gaps to evidence include direct ownership of digital initiatives, requirements leadership, business-case development, vendor / budget management, Agile delivery and hands-on familiarity with the named platforms.

A documentation contribution is not automatically equivalent to project delivery ownership. Describe only responsibilities actually performed.

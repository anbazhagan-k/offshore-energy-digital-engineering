### Certification cautions
- ISO 20816, ISO 13373 and ISO 55000 are standards referenced by Seadrill; the job description does not say that each is a personal certification requirement.
- CFIHOS, OSDU and Cognite Data Fusion are information standards / data platforms / ecosystems, not interchangeable personal certifications.
- Maximo, Azure, Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, Python, R, SQL and Power BI are tools or technologies. Training or vendor credentials may exist, but the vacancy does not name them as mandatory certifications.
- BOSIET / HUET requirements depend on the employer, operator, work location and travel duties. Do not obtain offshore safety training without confirming the required course and validity with the employer.
- Confirm current certification names, eligibility, training providers, exam rules and fees directly with the issuing organisations.

## 5. Suggested learning order

This is a suggested development sequence, not a commitment that completing courses guarantees eligibility.

1. Strengthen asset lifecycle, equipment hierarchy, EAM and maintenance data foundations.
2. Develop business analysis: requirements, process mapping, user stories and acceptance criteria.
3. Learn project delivery: scope, schedule, risk, governance, Agile and SDLC.
4. Build data skills: SQL, Power BI and practical data quality / dashboard work.
5. Study Azure fundamentals and energy data platform concepts.
6. Learn business case, cost-benefit, ROI and vendor evaluation methods.
7. Explore reliability engineering concepts: maintenance strategies, MTBF, MTTR, failure codes, FMEA and Weibull.
8. Pursue specialist vibration or lubricant credentials only if deliberately targeting reliability practitioner roles and after confirming access to appropriate practical training.

## 7. Application decision framework

- Orion-type digital project roles: prioritise when the vacancy values engineering communication, stakeholder requirements, digital project definition and technology coordination, and when direct delivery / commercial requirements are realistically supportable.
- Seadrill-type reliability roles: prioritise only when practical machinery maintenance and vibration-analysis requirements, offshore requirements and work-authorisation conditions are met.
- Hybrid EIM / digital engineering roles: use the overlap of asset data, EAM, information governance, digital delivery, analytics and stakeholder leadership as the most transferable direction.

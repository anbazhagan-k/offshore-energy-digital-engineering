# Offshore Energy Digital Engineering & Asset Management

> A structured learning, practice and portfolio repository for transitioning into offshore energy digital engineering and asset information management, with a focus on offshore platforms, subsea systems and LNG facilities.

## Repository purpose

This repository is the single source of truth for the learning journey: concepts, exercises, assessments, study records, practical projects and career preparation.

**Long-term career direction:** Digital Engineering and Asset Management Director  
**Target industry:** Offshore Oil & Gas and LNG  
**Technical focus:** Offshore platforms, subsea production systems, LNG facilities and asset lifecycle information  
**Learning approach:** Hybrid — sequential fundamentals, followed by integrated engineering, information and digital-systems learning  
**Progress method:** Markdown trackers  
**Repository visibility:** Private

## Learning principles

- Understand the physical asset before studying its information model.
- Understand the engineering information before studying the digital platform.
- Practise each concept using realistic but synthetic examples.
- Mark a lesson complete only after completing its exercise and assessment.
- Record uncertainties and revisit them rather than memorising unexplained terms.
- Keep employer-confidential, personal, export-controlled and proprietary information out of this repository.

## Lesson completion rule

A lesson is **COMPLETED** only when all of the following are true:

- [ ] Lesson material reviewed and understood.
- [ ] Practical exercise attempted and saved.
- [ ] Knowledge assessment completed.
- [ ] Mistakes and open questions recorded.
- [ ] Progress tracker updated.

## Status definitions

- `NOT STARTED` — not yet studied.
- `IN PROGRESS` — currently learning.
- `PRACTICE REQUIRED` — concept studied; exercise remains.
- `REVISION REQUIRED` — assessment or practical work exposed gaps.
- `COMPLETED` — completion criteria satisfied.

## Suggested weekly rhythm

Plan around 15 hours per week, adjusting when work or personal commitments require it.

| Activity | Suggested hours |

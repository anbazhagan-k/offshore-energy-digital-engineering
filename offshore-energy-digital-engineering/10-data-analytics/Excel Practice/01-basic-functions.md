## Concepts covered

Excel formulas begin with an equals sign (`=`). A function is a built-in operation that performs a calculation on supplied values or cell ranges.

| Function | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| SUM | Adds numbers | `=SUM(B2:B6)` |
| AVERAGE | Calculates arithmetic mean | `=AVERAGE(B2:B6)` |
| MAX | Returns the largest number | `=MAX(B2:B6)` |

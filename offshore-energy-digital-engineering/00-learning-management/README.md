# Learning Management

This folder controls the learning programme and records progress.

## Files

- [Learning roadmap](learning-roadmap.md) — phases, sequence and milestones.
- [Progress tracker](progress-tracker.md) — lesson-level status.
- [Study log](study-log.md) — dated record of study and practice.
- [Skills matrix](skills-matrix.md) — current and target capability.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md) — unresolved concepts to revisit.
- [Terms and definitions](terms-and-definitions.md) — cumulative glossary of terms from completed and reviewed study topics.
- [Review checklist](review-checklist.md) — weekly and monthly review routine.

## Update routine

At the end of every study session:
1. Add a dated entry to `study-log.md`.
2. Update the relevant row in `progress-tracker.md`.
3. Add unclear concepts to `open-questions.md`.
4. Update the skills matrix only when there is evidence of improvement.

At the end of each week, review completed work, identify the next lesson and set a realistic study target.

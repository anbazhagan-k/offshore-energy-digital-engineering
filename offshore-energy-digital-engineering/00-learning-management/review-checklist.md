# Review Checklist

## End-of-session review

- [ ] Record the date and time spent in `study-log.md`.
- [ ] Save notes and exercise files in the relevant module.
- [ ] Update lesson status in `progress-tracker.md`.
- [ ] Add unresolved questions to `open-questions.md`.
- [ ] Identify the next small learning task.

## Weekly review

- [ ] Review all sessions completed this week.
- [ ] Revisit difficult concepts without looking at notes.
- [ ] Complete at least one practical exercise.
- [ ] Update confidence ratings where evidence supports a change.
- [ ] Choose next week's learning objectives.

## Monthly review

- [ ] Summarise completed lessons and projects.
- [ ] Identify repeated mistakes and knowledge gaps.
- [ ] Review time spent against the intended study commitment.
- [ ] Check whether the learning sequence still matches target job descriptions.
- [ ] Record one measurable outcome for the next month.

## Quarterly review

- [ ] Review the phase gates in `learning-roadmap.md`.
- [ ] Reassess the skills matrix.
- [ ] Review target job descriptions and required platforms.
- [ ] Adjust learning priorities based on market evidence.
- [ ] Publish or refine a portfolio case study when appropriate.

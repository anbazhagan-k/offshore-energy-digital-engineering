# Skills Matrix

Use evidence, not familiarity, to rate each skill.

## Rating scale

1. Awareness — recognise terms and explain the broad purpose.
2. Beginner — explain basic concepts with guidance.
3. Working knowledge — complete standard tasks independently with references.
4. Proficient — handle varied tasks, troubleshoot and explain decisions.
5. Advanced — design approaches, guide others and lead implementation.

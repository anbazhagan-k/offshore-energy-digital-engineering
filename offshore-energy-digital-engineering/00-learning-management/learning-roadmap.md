# Learning Roadmap

## Programme objective

Develop practical capability for specialist roles in Offshore Energy Digital Engineering and Asset Information Management, progressing towards engineering data, digital delivery and asset lifecycle leadership.

**Study commitment:** approximately 15 hours/week  
**Planning horizon:** 18 months, reviewed every 3 months  
**Learning method:** hybrid

## Learning sequence

The programme begins with sequential engineering and industry fundamentals. Once the basic asset lifecycle and information concepts are understood, engineering, information management, digital platforms and analytics will be integrated through projects.

| Phase | Indicative period | Focus | Main evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Months 1–2 | Offshore energy fundamentals | Value-chain and lifecycle notes |
| 2 | Months 2–4 | Engineering fundamentals | Drawings and equipment exercises |
| 3 | Months 4–5 | Offshore platforms | Platform systems overview |
| 4 | Months 5–6 | Subsea systems | Subsea equipment register |
| 5 | Months 6–7 | LNG facilities | LNG process and equipment map |
| 6 | Months 7–9 | EIM and engineering data | Tag and document register |
| 7 | Months 9–11 | Asset management and ISO 55001 concepts | Asset lifecycle case study |
| 8 | Months 11–13 | Digital engineering and handover | Integrated handover model |
| 9 | Months 12–15 | EAM/CMMS concepts and platform exposure | Maintenance data model |
| 10 | Months 13–16 | Data analytics | Asset information dashboard |
| 11 | Months 15–18 | Integrated portfolio and career transition | Portfolio case studies and applications |

Periods overlap intentionally. The sequence is a guide, not a reason to delay job applications until the final month.

## Phase gates

### Gate 1 — Industry and asset literacy
- [ ] Explain upstream, midstream and downstream.
- [ ] Explain the offshore project and asset lifecycle.
- [ ] Identify major offshore facility systems.
- [ ] Explain the difference between platform, subsea and LNG facilities.

### Gate 2 — Engineering information literacy
- [ ] Read basic equipment tags and tag structures.
- [ ] Explain the purpose of a P&ID and equipment datasheet.
- [ ] Relate equipment records to drawings and documents.
- [ ] Explain revision, status and configuration control.

### Gate 3 — Asset information and management
- [ ] Explain asset hierarchy and functional location.
- [ ] Distinguish asset management from document management.
- [ ] Explain maintenance plans, work orders and equipment records.
- [ ] Describe information handover from project to operations.

### Gate 4 — Digital delivery and analytics
- [ ] Build a structured synthetic asset register.
- [ ] Perform data validation and completeness checks.
- [ ] Produce an asset information dashboard.
- [ ] Explain how engineering and EAM systems exchange information.

### Gate 5 — Career readiness
- [ ] Complete at least three portfolio case studies.
- [ ] Map skills to target job descriptions.
- [ ] Prepare a sector-focused CV and LinkedIn profile.
- [ ] Begin targeted applications and record feedback.

## Quarterly review

At the end of every 3 months, record:
- What was completed?
- What can now be explained without notes?
- Which exercises demonstrate practical capability?
- Which gaps are blocking progress?
- Should the sequence or target platform change based on real vacancies?
